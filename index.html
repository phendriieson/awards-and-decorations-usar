    About Us

    Quiet Professionals. Global Impact.

    USASOC emphasizes realism, discipline, and teamwork. Members have the opportunity to learn about special operations in an organized manner through training, coordination, and unit-based progression.

    Our community thrives on professionalism, communication, and continuous learning and improvement - which helps create an environment where everyone can develop skills, earn ranks, and participate in various events such as wargames, scrimmages, TPS, and much more.
